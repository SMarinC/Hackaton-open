## What the recording can establish

- **Observation:** COCO-SSD detects people in a historical reference clip. Temporary tracking and dwell are local observations. They do not establish purchases, demand, customer identity or a shortage.
- **Experimental comparison:** the new score compares horizontal image contrast with a manually captured reference. It samples the rectangular bounds of the configured zone, including any people or background there. A percentage is relative texture activity, not shelf fullness or remaining units.
- **Case and decision:** a dwell event opens a review case. Initial Slack `ask_review` is prepared only if the opening event includes a score below `0.6`. No reference or a higher score withholds that message. Capturing a reference or seeing a later drop does not automatically retry the decision for an existing case.
- **Inventory and closure:** the two branches use fictitious inventory and explicit local confirmation of a shelf issue. Case A closes only after assignment and a local replenishment report. Case B remains awaiting delivery information; it cannot close directly from `awaiting_delivery` with `restocked`.
- **Communication:** Slack and email are required by the intended D0. The recording session at `http://127.0.0.1:8790/index.html` uses an isolated database and retains `pending_connection` for both channels. Separately, the team supplied a screenshot showing a posted Slack message in `#panela-operaciones`. That evidence does not turn the recorded cases' prepared messages into sends or establish incoming replies, email delivery or autonomous follow-up.
